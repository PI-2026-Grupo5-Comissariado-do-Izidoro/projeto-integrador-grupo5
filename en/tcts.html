Identified Actions

In Toledo, human rights education gains strength through events of active citizenship and social inclusion. A recent milestone is the Municipal Human Rights Conference, promoted at the Jardim Europa Youth Center, focused on debates about racial equality, combating violence, and minority rights. Another practical example of guaranteeing fundamental rights is the "Paraná in Action" program, held at the Desirée Refosco Event Center, which provides free document issuance (CIN), legal guidance (Public Defender's Office), issuance of the card for autistic people (CIPTEA), and job vacancies, ensuring citizenship and dignity to the vulnerable population.

Identified Actions

In the municipality of Tupãssi, Paraná, there are actions that help the population and contribute to guaranteeing people's rights. One example is the work carried out by social assistance, which serves families who need support and guidance on benefits and social programs. Schools are also important in this process, as they teach students to respect differences, combat prejudice, and treat everyone equally. These actions are related to the Contemporary Cross-cutting Themes (TCTs), mainly Human Rights Education, because they help develop respect, citizenship, and social inclusion. In this way, residents can better understand their rights and learn to live more respectfully in society.

Identified Actions

The municipality of Nova Santa Rosa, in Paraná, develops integrated actions that promote human rights education, uniting citizenship, inclusion, and social protection. One of the main initiatives aimed at students in Nova Santa Rosa is the project Justice and Citizenship are also Learned in School, a partnership with the Court of Justice of Paraná for 5th-grade elementary students. The program works directly on awareness of rights and duties, resulting in textual productions and cultural activities on the subject, held annually. Furthermore, the city hall works to combat rights violations through the constant appointment and updating of members of its Child and Adolescent Protection Network, ensuring shelter and discussion institutions. The promotion of rights also expands to the socio-environmental awareness of the entire community through decentralized public events, such as the free screenings of the Cinema and Human Rights Showcase at the Municipal Public Library, which uses productions to debate equality, sustainability, and fundamental guarantees.
