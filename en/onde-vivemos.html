Where We Live

Gabriel

Municipality

Emancipated in 1951 from the colonization project of Fazenda Britânia (and later the Maripá colonizer), Toledo has consolidated itself as one of the main hubs of western Paraná. Located in a region of fertile lands (terra roxa), it has a gently undulating relief and a subtropical climate. It is recognized for its strength in the field, holding the title of Agribusiness Capital of Paraná, in addition to being nationally famous for its typical gastronomy, such as the Pig on the Spit Festival (Festa do Porco no Rolete), and for its beautiful postcards, such as the Diva Paim Barth Ecological Park (Municipal Lake).

Neighborhood/Line

Coopagro is one of the most representative neighborhoods in Toledo, symbolizing for me the strength of work, community union, and the constant growth of the city. What I value most about the neighborhood is its autonomy and dynamism: it has a strong local commerce, complete infrastructure, and practical services, without losing the welcoming neighborhood atmosphere. For me, Coopagro perfectly reflects the balance between quality of life, development, and the pride of belonging to a hardworking community.

Kévyn Danziger

Municipality

I live in Tupãssi, a city in the West of Paraná that is close to hubs like Toledo and Cascavel. Its history began in the late 1940s, when the first pioneers arrived to work with yerba mate and make room for agriculture. A curiosity is that, before actually being called Tupãssi, the city had different names, such as Gleba Quatro Lambari, Memória, and Colônia Pindorama.

Neighborhood/Line

I live in the district of Jotaesse, which began to be populated around 1957. The name comes from the initials of João Simões, a man who owned a lot of land around here in the past, being a united community focused on working with agriculture.

Adryan

Municipality

Nova Santa Rosa had its colonization started on January 13, 1953, by pioneers coming mostly from Santa Rosa (Rio Grande do Sul), with a strong presence of German and Italian descendants. The official foundation of the village occurred on September 19, 1954, and the political-administrative emancipation was carried out in the 1970s.
