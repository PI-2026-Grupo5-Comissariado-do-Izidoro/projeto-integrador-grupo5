Gabriel

Birth

Gabriel was born on January 29, 2011, in Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais. Bringing much happiness to our family, since then, our focus has been to present him with good examples and references to follow. We seek to transform his curiosity into real problem-solving.

School Trajectory

My academic journey began with strong family encouragement. My parents always prioritized investing in excellent education, which allowed me, from an early age, to understand the transformative value of study and discipline. This support was the foundation for me to develop my interest in the academic environment.

Historical Fact

On January 29, 2011, the date of my birth, the world witnessed a milestone in the struggle for fundamental rights: the Egyptian Revolution. This turning point in the Arab Spring consolidated civil resistance against dictatorial regimes, symbolizing the global quest for freedom, dignity, and social justice.

Current Moment

I am currently studying at the Federal Institute of Paraná, where I receive excellent education through specialized teachers, olympiads, and extension programs, providing me with an education that empowers me to become an ethical and responsible citizen.

Future Perspectives

I want to study Computer Engineering at UTFPR in 2028, building programs to contribute and add value through real projects that solve problems.
